Community Guidelines

Olamet Host Rules

Important rules and requirements for hosts to follow, whether you’re new or have a livestreaming experience.

1

No Multiple Accounts

Hosts are strictly prohibited from registering or operating multiple accounts.

2

Content Policy

Hosts must not showcase pornographic or explicit content during public live broadcasts. (Note: This restriction does not apply to private video calls).

3

Agency Integrity

Hosts must not transfer their account to another agent, nor should an agent induce/persuade a host to create their own independent agency on Olamet.

4

Age Requirement

All hosts must be 18 years of age or older to work on the platform.
